About Our Project

Using technology to create opportunities for rural entrepreneurs.

Who we are

Rural Startup Connect is a community engagement project designed to support small rural businesses through digital technology. Many entrepreneurs have useful products and skills but may lack access to digital promotion, online business information and technology education.


Our website provides a simple platform where entrepreneurs can list their businesses, learn digital skills and send enquiries for technology-related support.

Our objectives

🎯

Promote Local Businesses

Help rural entrepreneurs create an online presence and share business details.

📚

Improve Digital Literacy

Introduce practical technology, marketing and online safety skills.

🌱

Encourage Growth

Help small businesses discover resources and build confidence using technology.

Our vision

Our vision is to make digital technology easier to understand and use, so that rural entrepreneurs can share their skills, reach more customers and participate in the digital economy.
